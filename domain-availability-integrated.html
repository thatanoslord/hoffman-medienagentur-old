How to get the best domain name

1. Include your brand name−

Use your brand name or keywords to boost recognition and visibility in search engines.

2. Keep it short+

Short domain names are easier to remember, type and share.

3. Less is more+

Avoid hyphens, numbers and hard-to-spell words.

4. Think locally+

Consider a country-specific extension if you serve a local audience.

5. Domain availability search+

Check multiple extensions before settling on a final name.

6. Act fast+

Good domain names get taken quickly, so register yours as soon as you decide.
