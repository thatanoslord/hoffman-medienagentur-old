Overview

DPP READY helps companies begin preparing for the Digital Product Passport landscape with clarity. We translate a complex topic into practical next steps for your products, data, processes and digital foundations.

Our role is to support readiness and direction now: identifying what needs to be understood, where information is fragmented and what roadmap can help your company prepare with confidence. The focus is on creating a strong foundation before deadlines and technical requirements demand action.

Our process

We begin with a focused readiness conversation around your products, existing product information and the way data currently moves through your business. This helps identify the areas that need attention first.

We then map the key information, processes and digital systems involved. The aim is to create a clear picture of what exists today, what is missing and what may need to become more structured, connected or accessible.

From there, we create a practical DPP-readiness roadmap. It gives your business a clear sequence of next steps, priorities and decisions rather than leaving the subject as an abstract future compliance concern.

As requirements, product categories and technical options become clearer, the roadmap can evolve into the right implementation approach for your business.

What DPP READY includes

DPP READY can include a product-data and process review, readiness assessment, stakeholder alignment, digital-systems review and a practical implementation roadmap. The exact scope is shaped around your products, industry, existing information and business priorities.

We help you understand the opportunity and prepare the right foundation. Technical implementation is planned around confirmed product, regulatory and integration requirements.

Why preparation matters

New product-data requirements are easier to manage when they become part of a structured digital plan rather than an urgent compliance project. Starting early gives your business more time to understand its data, improve internal processes and make informed technology decisions.

DPP readiness can become more than a response to future requirements. It can help create better product information, clearer internal workflows and a stronger digital foundation for customers, partners and future growth.
